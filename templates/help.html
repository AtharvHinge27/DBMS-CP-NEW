Frequently Asked Questions

Navigate to Participation → My Teams and click Create Team. Select the open hackathon and name your team. InnoSphere calls the MySQL Stored Procedure sp_create_team_with_leader, which transactionally creates the team and generates a unique 8-character invitation code (e.g. INNO-NOMAD2). Share this code with your teammates so they can join using Team Invitations.

Profile completion is dynamically computed using the MySQL Stored Function fn_calculate_profile_completion(user_id). Each completed field contributes weight:
  • Roll Number: 10%
  • Department & Branch: 15%
  • Year & Semester: 10%
  • Phone Number: 10%
  • Technical Skills: 15%
  • Interests: 10%
  • GitHub, LinkedIn & Portfolio: 20%
  • Biography: 10%
Keep your profile updated under My Profile → Edit Profile to achieve 100% completion.

Internal Hackathons: Organized directly by your college or affiliated partner colleges (e.g. InnoHacks at PICT). Team formation, submissions, judging, and attendance are managed natively inside InnoSphere.
External Hackathons: Premier national competitions (e.g. Smart India Hackathon, KAVACH, RBI HarBinger, Google Solution Challenge). InnoSphere tracks your application approval status while directing you to the verified official portal.

As configured in evaluation_criteria, each project is graded across 4 rubrics, each worth 25 points (Total 100 points):
  1. Innovation & Originality (25 pts): Novelty of idea and creative problem-solving.
  2. Feasibility & Impact (25 pts): Practical applicability and societal/commercial utility.
  3. Technical Execution (25 pts): Code quality, database schema normalization, and API resilience.
  4. Presentation & Demo (25 pts): Clarity of pitch, UI responsiveness, and live working prototype.
Trigger trg_before_evaluation_insert automatically validates that scores remain within bounds.

Navigate to Performance → Attendance to review your verified check-in timestamps. If your attendance status shows "Absent" or "Pending", contact your college coordinator with proof of participation to request a status correction.
